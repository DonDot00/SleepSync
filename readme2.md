# SleepSync
An Ai-assistant scheduling tool that optimizes your calendar around your sleep so you perform at your best, every day.

# What is SleepSync?
SleepSync is an AI-powered assistant that analyzes your schedule and helps you build positive habits around optimal sleep tailored to you. Instead of the nuances of daily life eat into your rest and relaxation, Sleep Sync meticulously plans your day with you sleep health as it's main priority. 

Whether your striving to meet a deadline or manging a packed week, SleepSync makes sure you're not sacrificing the sleep you need to efficiently get things done.
